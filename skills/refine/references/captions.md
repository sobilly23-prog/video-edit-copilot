# Captions

The taste profile sets the voice and look. These are the defaults for long-form video, with the reasons they exist.
For short-form (vertical), text sits in the upper-middle safe band instead: see "Short-form" in `video-types.md`.

## Every caption has one job, or it goes

- **Orient:** where we are (a place card) or where we're going (a bridge line).
- **Inform:** the exact, verified name of a place, dish, drink or thing, on its own shot. This is what makes a video
  worth saving.
- **Connect:** a short personal note in the user's own words, used rarely.

**A caption that only describes what's already on screen gets deleted** ("a shelf of mugs"). Narration of what you're
doing gets deleted too ("here's me walking to the next place").

## Wording

- **Plain and precise beats clever:** "Bronze bust of the founder", not "so impressive!". Name the brew method
  correctly ("iced pour-over", not "iced coffee").
- **Dish and drink labels:** the name, then at most one familiar comparison or texture ("Like a cinnamon roll, but
  flaky like a croissant").
- **Name meals correctly:** a stop where you only had a pastry is a snack, not lunch. Check what was eaten.
- **Bridges say what the next stop is *for*:** "Back inside for dessert", not "One last stop".
- **If the bridge shot shows something notable,** mention it and still point ahead ("More street art on the way to
  lunch").
- **Personal notes are the user's.** Draft them only as options for the user to approve or rewrite.
- **When a place appears that you'll visit properly later,** say so ("We'll come back here tomorrow"). Check *when* in
  the footage.
- **Don't run the same line three times in one film.** Vary it.
- **Capitalise the first letter.** Proper nouns as the place spells them.
- **No calendar dates on screen** if the user prefers chapter names ("Day 3").

## Place cards

- **Name, plus the full address:** street, district, city, postcode, verified on a map.
- **In malls and food halls, add the floor, spelled out** ("G Floor", "4th Floor").
- **A stall inside a food hall:** the venue card first, then the stall card with one fact as its second line.
- **Unsure of a name or address?** Dig into the official site, the map listing's photos, menus and delivery apps. Then
  give the user options with sources, rather than guessing.

## Timing

- **On screen for at least max(3 s, characters ÷ 15).** If the shot under it is shorter, lengthen the shot or let the
  caption continue over the next shot of the same subject.
- **Start on the cut and end on a cut.** Snap anything within about 0.6 s.
- **Never two captions at once.** A card's name and address count as one unit.
- **A caption longer than its shot pushes every later caption late.** Check each caption's minimum against its shot
  before building.

## Look (defaults; the profile overrides)

- **Small, editorial, consistent.** One style across the whole film.
- **Place cards** lower-left. Other captions centred at the bottom, like subtitles.
- **A quick fade in** (fully on within about 0.7 s), no other animation. Some apps' default "fade in" spreads the
  fade over the caption's whole length, so it's only fully visible on its last frame. Check how the app's fade
  actually behaves.
- **A soft background pill** helps on bright footage. Some apps ignore the pill colour's transparency, so lower the
  caption's own opacity instead (about 0.8).
- **Check every caption is centred before handing over** where it should be. Reviewers notice off-centre captions
  straight away.
